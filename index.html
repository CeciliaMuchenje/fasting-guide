How to use this guide

Daily pattern (30-60 minutes)
  • Thank God first: favor, protection, life.
  • Read the passages slowly, aloud if you can.
  • Pray the day's prayer, then pray in your own words.
  • Worship with a song, then be still for 5 minutes to listen.
  • Write what God shows you, including your dreams.
Fasting options (choose what suits your health)
  • Level 1: Partial fast, no food from 6am to 12pm or 3pm, then a simple meal.
  • Level 2: Daniel fast: vegetables, fruit, water; no meat, sweets or snacks.
  • Level 3: Full-day water fast on selected days only (for example Wednesdays and Fridays).
  • Fasting beyond food: social media, entertainment, anything feeding temptation.
If you are unwell, pregnant, diabetic, or on medication, speak with your doctor first. God honors a sincere heart, so you can fast from media instead of food.
Keeping your freedom (practical)
  • Remove or block what feeds sexual temptation: certain apps, sites, chats, late-night scrolling.
  • Do not fight alone: tell one mature believer or pastor who will pray with you (James 5:16).
  • Before sleep: pray Psalm 91, thank God, and keep your phone away from your bed.
  • Recurring distressing dreams can also be linked to stress and poor sleep, so rest, eat well and seek counsel if they continue.
Final declaration of faith

I declare that I am a child of God, redeemed by the blood of Jesus. Every curse is broken. Every altar is destroyed. Every evil dream is canceled. Sexual sin has no power over me. The Holy Spirit fills me and leads me. I walk in divine health, favor and protection. My destiny is secure in Christ, and I will fulfil every purpose God has for me. In Jesus' mighty name. Amen.

Philippians 1:6: He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.
Quick-access prayer points

Against sexual sin

  • I break every addiction to pornography, masturbation and lustful thoughts.
  • I renounce every sexual relationship outside of marriage.
  • Every spirit of lust, I command you to leave me now.
  • I am dead to sin and alive to righteousness (Romans 6:11).

Against evil dreams

  • Every dream of being chased, I cancel you now.
  • Every dream of sexual defilement, I cancel you now.
  • My dreams are from God: dreams of peace, direction and victory.

For destiny helpers

  • Father, connect me to the right people at the right time.
  • I break every relationship that delays my destiny.
  • I declare divine connections and godly mentors in my life.

For protection

  • I cover myself and my family with the blood of Jesus.
  • We dwell in the secret place of the Most High (Psalm 91).
  • No evil shall befall us, no plague shall come near our dwelling.
Closing prayer for each day

"In the name of Jesus, I thank You, Lord. I receive Your Holy Spirit, Your peace and Your protection over me and my family. Amen."
